# Content integrity — Level 3 (R10)

This gate takes precedence over nonmaterial brevity and cosmetic preferences.

## Decide what may be removed
- **Material:** anything affecting a decision, interpretation, accountability, compliance, financial/technical accuracy, risk, scope, operating instructions, deadlines, source traceability or user-stated requirements. **Preserve.**
- **Peripheral:** repeated prose, redundant transitions, unnecessary decorative explanations and non-actionable details with no effect on comprehension or decisions. **May condense or omit.**
- **Uncertain:** if unsure whether content is material, keep it or ask. Do not silently guess.
- **Missing:** mark unavailable data and avoid invented numbers, timelines, names or claims.

## Validation method
1. Inventory the source's claims, quantitative values, identifiers, dates, assumptions and required actions.
2. Map the source items to the target artifact. Ensure all material items have a destination.
3. Recalculate totals where inputs are available; show units, timeframes, denominators and comparisons.
4. Make assumptions explicitly distinguishable from source facts and interpretations.
5. For strict R03/R04 conflicts: ask for a user decision on scope/layout before removing material information.
6. For a repair, compare the revised artifact against the original for omissions and regressions; the word-count reduction is **not** a success metric.

Output quality is assessed on correct comprehension and preserved material facts, not how little text is left.
