# Catch up — Level 2 workflow

Source: original SKILL.md §2; the form instruction below is reproduced verbatim.

| The reader needs to… | Build | It looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Catch up (weekly or monthly update) | A report | The 3 or 4 numbers that matter as tiles, each with its change since last time in green or red. One chart. The decisions you need as cards. Then what happened. |

Original routing principle: "The job decides the form, and the form decides the layout. A report, a decision page and a guide must not share one skeleton of tiles, a box and tabs. Same Claude look, different layouts."
