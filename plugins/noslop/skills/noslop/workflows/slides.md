# Say yes — Level 2 workflow

Source: original SKILL.md §2; the form instruction below is reproduced verbatim.

| The reader needs to… | Build | It looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Say yes (pitch, proposal, plan for a group) | Slides | One idea per slide. Each slide is one big visual (a number, a chart, a before and after, a diagram) and one sentence. Arrow keys and buttons. The last slide is the ask. |

Original routing principle: "The job decides the form, and the form decides the layout. A report, a decision page and a guide must not share one skeleton of tiles, a box and tabs. Same Claude look, different layouts."

Read `../references/design-rules.md` before building; read `../references/style-kit.md` before implementing HTML; read `../references/quality-checks.md` before delivery. All referenced original rules remain mandatory.
